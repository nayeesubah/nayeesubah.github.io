The Community Solar Energy Plant is one of our flagship investment projects. Located on a 2-acre plot on the outskirts of the city, this 500kW solar installation powers over 200 households while generating revenue through the grid feed-in tariff.

**Key Highlights:**
- 500kW installed capacity
- 200+ households powered
- 25-year power purchase agreement
- Annual maintenance contract with leading solar OEM
- Carbon credit eligible

**Impact:**
- Reduces carbon footprint by approximately 600 tonnes CO2 annually
- Creates 5 direct jobs for local community members
- Provides stable, predictable returns for the foundation
