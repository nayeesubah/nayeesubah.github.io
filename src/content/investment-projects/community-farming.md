The Community Farming Cooperative unites 50 local farmers to cultivate organic vegetables, fruits, and herbs on 10 acres of collectively managed farmland. The cooperative model ensures fair prices, shared resources, and sustainable farming practices.

**Operations:**
- 10 acres of certified organic farmland
- 50 member farmers
- Direct-to-consumer supply chain
- Organic vegetable box subscription service
- Training center for sustainable agriculture

**Community Benefits:**
- Farmer incomes increased by 40%
- Access to premium organic markets
- Shared irrigation and equipment
- Training in modern farming techniques
