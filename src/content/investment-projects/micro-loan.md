The Qard Hasan Micro-Loan Program provides completely interest-free credit to micro-entrepreneurs who would otherwise have no option but predatory moneylenders charging exploitative rates. In line with Islamic principles, Nayee Subah Foundation charges no interest (riba) of any kind, ever — borrowers repay only the exact amount they borrowed.

**How It Works — Qard Hasan (Interest-Free Loan):**
- Borrowers receive a loan and repay only the principal — zero interest, zero hidden fees
- A revolving fund model: as loans are repaid, the same capital is redeployed to the next family in need, multiplying the impact of every rupee donated
- Group lending model built on social trust and mutual accountability, not collateral or interest
- Free financial literacy and halal business training included with every loan

**Program Details:**
- Average loan size: ₹25,000
- Interest rate: **0% — always.** This is a core principle of the Foundation and is never compromised.
- Repayment period: 6–24 months, principal only
- Any processing fee is a small, flat amount capped to cover real administrative cost only — never tied to loan size or duration, and never treated as profit

**Why Interest-Free Matters:**
Many small entrepreneurs in rural India are trapped by informal moneylenders charging 3–5% *per month* — debt that spirals rather than helps. This program exists specifically to break that cycle: by relieving borrowers of the burden of interest, we give them a genuine chance to build a business and lift their families out of poverty, not just service a debt.

**Achievements:**
- 500+ interest-free loans disbursed to date
- 98% repayment rate — proof that dignity and trust outperform predatory lending
- 85% of borrowers are women
- 300+ new businesses created, debt-free from day one
