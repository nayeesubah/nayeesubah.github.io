The Micro Loan Program provides affordable credit to micro-entrepreneurs who lack access to traditional banking services. By providing small loans at reasonable interest rates, we empower individuals to start or expand their businesses.

**Program Details:**
- Average loan size: ₹25,000
- Interest rate: 12% per annum (well below market rate)
- Repayment period: 6-24 months
- Group lending model for social collateral
- Financial literacy training included

**Achievements:**
- 500+ loans disbursed to date
- 98% repayment rate
- 85% of borrowers are women
- 300+ new businesses created
