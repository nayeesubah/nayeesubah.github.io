Small farmers in rural Jharkhand often lose most of their margin to middlemen before produce ever reaches a buyer. Our Helping Local Farmers program shortens that chain so growers keep more of what their harvest is actually worth.

- Organizing regular farmer's markets so growers sell straight to buyers, no middleman
- Farm-direct supply arrangements connecting local farmers with nearby institutions and households
- Fair-price guidance so farmers know the true market rate before they sell
- Basic support on storage and post-harvest handling to reduce spoilage losses
- Encouraging community members to choose local, farmer-sourced produce

Twelve markets now run regularly across the district, giving over 200 farming families a fairer, more direct route from field to table.
