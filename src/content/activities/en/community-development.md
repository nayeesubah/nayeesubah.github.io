Our Community Development program works at the grassroots level to create lasting change. We focus on:

- **Clean Water Access**: Installing hand pumps and water filtration systems in 15 villages
- **Sanitation**: Building community toilets and promoting hygiene awareness
- **Infrastructure**: Supporting local road repair, community center construction, and solar lighting
- **Livelihoods**: Training programs for sustainable agriculture, handicrafts, and small businesses
- **Community Mobilization**: Forming village development committees to drive local change
