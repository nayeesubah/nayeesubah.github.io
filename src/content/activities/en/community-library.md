Our Community Library program brings free, welcoming reading spaces to villages where children have never had access to a proper library. Each library is stocked and run to help students build a genuine reading habit — not just cram for exams.

- Free access to textbooks, storybooks, and reference material for all ages
- Dedicated competitive-exam section for board exams, engineering and medical entrance tests
- Quiet, well-lit reading spaces open after school hours and on weekends
- Local volunteers staff the library and mentor students on study habits
- Regular book drives to keep the collection growing

What started as a single room in Giridih has grown into a trusted second home for hundreds of students who come back day after day — proof that access, not just intent, is what changes outcomes.
