Our Education for All program aims to ensure that every child has access to quality education. We provide:

- School supplies and uniforms
- After-school tutoring
- Scholarships for meritorious students
- Computer literacy programs
- Career counseling

Through this initiative, we have helped over 500 children continue their education.
