On March 10, 2025, Nayee Subah Foundation celebrated a milestone moment — the inauguration of its first community library in Giridihdistrict of Jharkhand. What began as a simple idea to bridge the educational divide has become a vibrant hub of learning, curiosity, and opportunity for hundreds of children and adults alike.

Established in partnership with local village leaders and community volunteers, the library stands as a testament to what collective effort can achieve. For the children of Dumka's remote villages, this is more than a building filled with books — it is a doorway to a wider world.

## A Space Designed for Learning and Discovery

The library houses over **2,000 carefully curated books** spanning subjects from mathematics and science to literature, history, and environmental studies. But the vision went far beyond printed pages. The facility includes four computer terminals with high-speed internet access, allowing students to explore digital learning resources, take online courses, and prepare for competitive examinations that were previously out of reach.

"Our children had to travel over 20 kilometers to access a proper library," says Sunita Devi, a local school teacher who has been volunteering with the foundation for three years. "Most families cannot afford that time or expense. This library will transform educational opportunities for our entire community."

## Early Impact: A Community Embraces Its Library

Within the first week of opening, the library registered over **500 children as members** — far exceeding initial projections. The response underscores a deep, unmet need for educational resources in the region.

The library operates on a flexible schedule designed to serve the whole community:

- **Morning hours (6–8 AM):** Open for working adults and daily wage laborers who wish to read newspapers, government exam materials, or skill-development books before heading to work.
- **School hours (9 AM–3 PM):** Reserved for schoolchildren to access reference materials, complete homework, and use the computer lab.
- **Afternoon programs (3–6 PM):** After-school tutoring in mathematics, science, and English, led by volunteer educators from nearby towns.
- **Evening classes (6–8 PM):** Adult literacy programs and vocational workshops, including basic computer skills and financial literacy.

## Beyond Books: A Community Hub

The library has quickly evolved into a true community center. Weekend workshops have drawn enthusiastic participation:

- **Spoken English and communication skills** sessions for youth preparing for jobs and higher education.
- **Digital literacy workshops** teaching villagers how to use government service portals, mobile banking, and online job applications.
- **Storytelling and creative writing** sessions for children, led by local authors and educators.
- **Health awareness talks** in partnership with local healthcare workers.

> "I never thought I would see a library in my village. My daughter now spends hours reading storybooks and practicing on the computer. She wants to become a teacher one day." — Ram Prasad, parent of a library member

## Looking Ahead: A Network of Learning Centers

This community library is the first of five planned across Jharkhand over the next two years. Each library will be tailored to the specific needs of its community, with local committees overseeing daily operations to ensure long-term sustainability.

The foundation is also exploring partnerships with educational technology platforms to provide free access to online courses, virtual classrooms, and digital certifications for youth and adults.

"We believe that access to knowledge is a fundamental right," said the foundation's coordinator during the inauguration ceremony. "Every child, regardless of their economic background, deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and build a better future. This library is our promise to that belief."
