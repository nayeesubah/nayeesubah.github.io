On April 5, 2025, Nayee Subah Foundation inaugurated a new Skill Development Center in the Godda district of Jharkhand — a major step forward in our mission to create sustainable livelihood opportunities for rural youth.

The center, located in the heart of Godda town, is designed to address one of the most pressing challenges facing the region: **high youth unemployment driven by a lack of market-relevant skills**.

## The Challenge: Youth Unemployment in Rural Jharkhand

Jharkhand has one of the highest youth unemployment rates in India. According to the Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE), the state's youth unemployment rate (age 15–29) stood at **over 25%** in 2024. The problem is particularly acute in rural districts like Godda, where formal employment opportunities are scarce and most young people lack access to quality vocational training.

"After finishing school, I spent two years sitting at home," says Rajesh Kumar, a 19-year-old from a village near Godda. "There were no jobs, and I had no skills that anyone would pay for. I felt hopeless."

Rajesh is now among the first batch of students at the new Skill Development Center, enrolled in the electrician and basic electronics course.

## Courses Offered

The center launched with three flagship vocational programs, each designed in consultation with local employers to ensure graduates have the skills the market actually demands:

### 1. Tailoring and Garment Making (6 months)

- **Capacity:** 60 students per batch
- **Curriculum:** Pattern making, cutting, stitching, embroidery, machine maintenance
- **Career pathways:** Self-employment (tailoring shop), garment factory worker, tailoring instructor
- **Certification:** Recognized by Jharkhand Skill Development Mission (JSDM)

### 2. Computer Skills and Digital Literacy (3 months)

- **Capacity:** 80 students per batch
- **Curriculum:** MS Office, internet and email, digital payments, online government services, basics of graphic design and social media marketing
- **Career pathways:** Data entry operator, retail assistant, office assistant, freelancer
- **Certification:** Foundation-certified with NIELIT alignment

### 3. Electrician and Basic Electronics (6 months)

- **Capacity:** 60 students per batch
- **Curriculum:** Domestic and industrial wiring, electrical safety, motor repair, solar panel installation, basic electronics
- **Career pathways:** Licensed electrician, construction company employee, solar technician, self-employment
- **Certification:** Recognized by JSDM and partnering with local electrical contractors for apprenticeship placement

### All courses are completely free of cost and include:

- High-quality course materials and tools
- Hands-on practical training
- Soft skills and workplace communication training
- Industry-recognized certification
- Placement assistance upon completion

## Facilities and Infrastructure

The center is equipped with modern training infrastructure:

- **Dedicated computer lab** with 20 networked workstations and high-speed internet.
- **Tailoring workshop** with 30 industrial sewing machines and fabric inventory.
- **Electrical workshop** with wiring boards, test equipment, solar panel demonstration units, and safety gear.
- **Classroom spaces** for theory sessions, with audio-visual teaching aids.
- **Library and resource room** with reference books and online learning access.
- **Counseling office** for career guidance and placement support.

## Industry Partnerships for Placement

A key differentiator of this center is its strong focus on employability. The foundation has established partnerships with:

- **Local construction companies** for electrician and general worker placements.
- **Ready-made garment businesses** in Dumka and Bhagalpur for tailoring graduates.
- **Retail chains and telecom companies** for computer-skills graduates.
- **Solar energy companies** expanding into rural Jharkhand for trained technicians.

Graduates receive a certificate jointly recognized by Nayee Subah Foundation and the **Jharkhand Skill Development Mission (JSDM)**, adding credibility to their qualifications.

> "We don't just want to train youth — we want to place them in jobs or help them start their own businesses. Every student who walks through these doors should leave with a clear path to a livelihood." — Center Director, Godda Skill Development Center

## Student Spotlight: Rajesh's Journey

Rajesh Kumar, 19, is one of the first 60 students in the electrician program. His father works as a daily wage laborer, earning ₹300–400 on days when work is available. The family of five survives on less than ₹8,000 per month.

"I completed my 12th grade but had no skills and no connections to find a job," Rajesh explains. "When I heard about this center offering free training with a job guarantee, I didn't believe it at first. But here I am — learning practical skills every day."

Rajesh's goal is to complete the course, work with a construction company for two years to gain experience, and then start his own electrical repair business in his village.

## Expansion Plans

The Godda center is the pilot for a larger vision. Based on its performance, the foundation plans to:

- Open similar centers in **Pakur and Sahebganj districts** within the next 18 months.
- Expand the course catalog to include **beauty and wellness, mobile phone repair, and hospitality** based on market demand.
- Establish a **placement cell** that maintains relationships with employers across Jharkhand and Bihar.
- Offer **evening and weekend batches** for working youth who want to upgrade their skills.
- Launch an **entrepreneurship track** for graduates who wish to start their own businesses, with access to the foundation's revolving loan fund.

Each center aims to train at least **200 youth annually**, with a target placement rate of 70% within six months of graduation.
