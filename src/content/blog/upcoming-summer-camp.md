Summer break in rural India often means one thing for children: months away from structured learning, with limited opportunities for enrichment. For many, it is a time of boredom, idle hours, or — worse — engagement in child labor to support family income.

Nayee Subah Foundation's **Summer Learning Camp 2025** aims to change that. Designed as a fun, safe, and enriching space for children aged 6 to 14, the camp will run from **May 15 to June 10, 2025** (4 weeks) across three locations in Jharkhand: **Dumka, Godda, and Sahibganj**.

## A Camp Designed for Holistic Development

The camp curriculum goes far beyond traditional summer school. Our program combines academic reinforcement, creative exploration, physical activity, and essential life skills into a single integrated experience.

### Academic Support

Children often lose ground academically during the long summer break — a phenomenon known as the "summer slide." Our remedial classes help bridge that gap:

- **Mathematics:** Foundational skills, mental math, and problem-solving through games.
- **Science:** Hands-on experiments and nature observation.
- **Language:** Hindi, English, and Santhali — reading, writing, and storytelling.
- **Environmental Studies:** Understanding local ecology, agriculture, and conservation.

Classes are conducted in small groups (15–20 children per instructor) to ensure individual attention.

### Creative Arts

Every child deserves the chance to create. Our arts program includes:

- **Painting and drawing** — using natural materials like clay, leaves, and vegetable dyes.
- **Music and dance** — traditional folk songs, tribal dances, and introduction to musical instruments.
- **Storytelling and drama** — children write and perform short plays based on local stories and social themes.
- **Handicrafts** — making useful items from recycled and locally available materials.

### Sports and Physical Activity

Physical health and teamwork are integral to the camp experience:

- **Traditional Indian games:** Kabaddi, kho-kho, gilli-danda, and pithu garam.
- **Modern sports:** Football, volleyball, badminton, and athletics.
- **Yoga and mindfulness:** Daily morning sessions for concentration and emotional well-being.
- **Obstacle courses and team challenges** that build confidence and cooperation.

### Life Skills

Perhaps the most important component — skills that last a lifetime:

- **First aid and basic health:** Recognizing common illnesses, handwashing, and when to seek help.
- **Personal hygiene and sanitation:** Making the practice habitual through fun activities.
- **Environmental awareness:** Tree planting, waste segregation, and water conservation.
- **Teamwork and leadership:** Group projects that require planning, negotiation, and collective problem-solving.

### Field Trips

Learning extends beyond the camp premises. Planned excursions include:

- Visits to local farms to understand agriculture and food systems.
- Trips to nearby factories and small-scale industries.
- Visits to historical sites and natural landmarks in the region.

## Volunteer Opportunities

We are looking for passionate individuals to join us as camp volunteers. Positions include:

- **Camp counselors** (10 per location) — supervise and engage with children throughout the day.
- **Activity coordinators** (5 per location) — lead specific activities like sports, arts, or music.
- **Support staff** (5 per location) — assist with meals, logistics, and first aid.

Volunteers should be at least 18 years old, fluent in Hindi (Santhali is a plus), and available for the full 4-week duration. Accommodation and meals will be provided.
