Nayee Subah Foundation organized a comprehensive free health camp in the Dumka district on March 20, 2025, bringing essential medical services to over 500 patients from 12 surrounding villages.

## Services Provided

- **General checkups:** 500+ patients examined for common ailments
- **Eye examinations:** 200 people screened, 60 pairs of glasses distributed
- **Dental checkups:** 150 patients received dental care and hygiene counseling
- **Child immunization:** 80 children received their scheduled vaccinations
- **Free medicines:** Over ₹1 lakh worth of medicines were distributed free of cost

## Impact

The camp identified 15 individuals requiring specialized follow-up care, who have been referred to partner hospitals. Health awareness sessions on hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention reached over 300 women and children.
