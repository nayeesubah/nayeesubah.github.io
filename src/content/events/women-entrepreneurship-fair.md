Nayee Subah Foundation is organizing a Women Entrepreneurship Fair to provide a market platform for women entrepreneurs trained under our skill development program.

## Event Highlights

- **50+ stalls** featuring handmade products, garments, food items, and crafts
- **Live demonstrations** of traditional crafts and food processing
- **Business mentoring** sessions by successful women entrepreneurs
- **Networking opportunities** with local businesses and suppliers

## Who Should Attend

- Local buyers and retailers looking for quality handmade products
- NGOs and government agencies working on women's empowerment
- Anyone interested in supporting women-led businesses

## Participation

Interested women entrepreneurs from our program can register for a stall by September 20. For inquiries, contact us at nsfgiridih@gmail.com.
