Nayee Subah Foundation invites you to its Annual Day 2025 — a celebration of a year dedicated to education, community service, and positive change across rural Jharkhand.

## What to Expect

### Cultural Programs
Students from our community library and scholarship programs will showcase traditional folk dances, songs, and skits that highlight the cultural heritage of the Santhal Pargana region.

### Success Stories
Hear directly from the beneficiaries whose lives have been transformed — from first-generation learners to women entrepreneurs who launched their own businesses through our programs.

### Volunteer & Member Recognition
We will honor the dedicated volunteers, donors, and members whose unwavering support made our work possible. Certificates and mementos will be presented.

### Future Plans
Our founder Mohammed Irfan will share the roadmap for FY 2025–26, including new community libraries, the mobile health clinic project, and expanded scholarship programs.
